# Company financial health

Use Maple's audited public-web route (`maple-web search`, `read`, and `fetch`) to locate original investor-relations results and SEC filings. Search snippets are discovery aids, not verification. If a PDF cannot be extracted reliably, seek the issuer's corresponding SEC HTML filing and disclose remaining gaps.

Identify the as-of date, fiscal quarter, period end, release date, currency and units. Check the issuer's results listing for newer reports. Cite primary-source URLs next to material figures; distinguish reported facts, calculations and management forecasts.

Explain revenue growth and its main drivers, operating cash flow, cash capital spending, liquidity and debt. Check whether cash-flow statements cover a quarter or cumulative fiscal year. Derive a standalone quarter only from verified, consistently scoped cumulative figures; otherwise label the annual or year-to-date measure and leave quarterly values unknown. Do not mix periods in ratios or annualize a seasonal quarter without qualification.

When using free cash flow, show operating cash flow minus cash purchases of property and equipment and label it as a non-GAAP calculation. Keep capex including finance leases separate from cash capex. Reconcile debt's current and non-current portions plus commercial paper without double counting; distinguish carrying value from principal and fair value. State which cash investments and lease liabilities are included or excluded in any net-cash comparison.

Return a concise financial-health interpretation, source-linked figures, and uncertainties. Flag material one-off benefits and explain why cash generation, liquidity or obligations qualify the headline growth. Do not infer solvency, investment attractiveness or future returns from one quarter. Research does not authorize trades, account access or publication of private financial information.
