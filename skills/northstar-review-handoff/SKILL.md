For a Northstar demo review handoff, record the demo artifact link, the supplied
synthetic next reviewer, and the review status. Ask for the reviewer when none
is supplied and mark the assignment pending until confirmed. Mark missing or
unverified review outcomes as pending; naming a reviewer does not establish
acceptance or approval. Use only synthetic information. This is a Northstar
demo convention, not a rule for other organizations.
